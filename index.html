Bio

Hello! My name is Ping'an He. Welcome to my page, I try to make it interesting! 🙌 I am currently a PhD candidate in Probability and Mathematical Statistics at Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University (BNBU), pursuing a PhD degree of Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU), under the supervision of Prof. Huajun Ye and Prof. Kai-Tai Fang . From May 2026 to July 2026, I was a visiting student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, hosted by Prof. Xinyuan Song . I received my Master's degree from Nankai University in 2022 and my Bachelor's degree from Xinjiang University in 2019.

My research interests focus on multivariate statistical analysis and statistical representative points. At the same time, I enjoy mathematical derivation and aim to extend my research through code! In life, I love running and table tennis, which make me feel free and happy! ✉️ Feel free to contact me if you would like to connect for any fun ideas or collaboration! I’m always eager to learn more and expand my horizons!

Research Projects

  • Project 1: Risk Assessment and Sampling Design for Vegetable Safety in Shenzhen

    Description: Used multivariate statistical methods (Safety Index, PCA, TOPSIS) to assess risk in vegetable supply channels across Shenzhen and designed an improved sampling scheme that reduces error.

  • Project 2: Using Kalman Filter to Predict Wind Speed

    Description: Applied Kalman filtering to predict wind speed for wind farms in Hami, Xinjiang, and demonstrated superior accuracy compared with standard time-series forecasts.

  • Project 3: Copula and Its Statistical Representative Points

    Description: Design sampling methods for common copula distributions and conduct related theoretical research.
